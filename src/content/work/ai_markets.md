RAGGuard sits in front of the retrieval step in a RAG pipeline and evaluates each retrieved document against a set of poisoning indicators before it reaches the model's context.

Detection is multimodal: embedding-space anomaly detection, style consistency scoring against the corpus baseline, and privilege tier validation for multi-tenant corpora. Documents that fail validation are quarantined and flagged for human review.
