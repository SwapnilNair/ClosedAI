## Intent

ClosedAI is a small piece of internet real estate that I control. It is designed for long-form technical writing and durable project records rather than an algorithmic feed.

## Constraints

The site should stay static, fast, readable, and easy to publish from a text editor. Articles live as Markdown or MDX in the repository, with typed frontmatter, generated archives, topic pages, RSS, and no database to maintain.

## Design decision

The interface borrows from operational software: structured metadata, diagrams, fine-line grids, and restrained contrast. The visual system is meant to make evidence easier to scan, not to imitate a fictional command center.
